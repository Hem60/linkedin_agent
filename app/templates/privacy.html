Privacy

What PostPilot stores, why, and how to get rid of it.

What is stored

Only what's needed to write posts that sound like you and publish them.

  • From LinkedIn: your name, your email, and your LinkedIn member id.
  • From you: your role, the topics you post about, your preferred tone and timezone. Business accounts also give a company name, products, markets and buyer types.
  • Your posts: topics, drafts, edits, hashtags, schedules and outcomes.
  • Your images: pictures you upload or pick, stored under your account only.
  • A permission to post: a LinkedIn access token, encrypted before it is saved.

What is not stored

  • Your LinkedIn password. Sign-in happens on LinkedIn; this app never sees it.
  • Your connections, messages, feed, or anyone else's data.
  • Payment details. There are none.

Who else sees it

Three services, each given only the piece it needs to do its job.

  • Google Gemini and Groq receive the topic you type and your role and niche, in order to write the drafts. Free model tiers may use prompts to improve their products — don't type anything confidential.
  • Pexels receives a two or three word search phrase. Nothing else.
  • LinkedIn receives the post you approve, when you publish it.

Your data is never sold, and never shared with anyone else.

Nothing is published without you

Every post is written, previewed and approved by you before it goes anywhere. There is no automatic posting. A scheduled post publishes at the time you picked, from the draft you approved.

Leaving

You can take your data with you or erase it, at any time, from your account page. Deleting removes your posts, your images and your LinkedIn permission. It cannot be undone, and it is a real delete — nothing is kept behind a flag.

Posts already published to LinkedIn stay on LinkedIn. Remove those there.
